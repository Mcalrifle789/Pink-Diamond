By default Pink Diamond restricts inappropriate material: no sexual or sexually explicit content, no graphic gore, and no content meant to harass, demean or frighten. Requests for that material are declined.

Adults who add Uncensored Mode may create mature fiction, profanity, dark themes and sexual content involving fictional adults, and get blunter answers. You are responsible for what you create and how you use it.
